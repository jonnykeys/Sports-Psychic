# Sports Psychic

A competitive sports prediction app where users forecast the outcomes of sporting events and compete against friends in seasonal or week-to-week league formats.

## Features

- **Pre-Season Predictions**: Make predictions on sporting event outcomes before the season begins
- **League Competitions**: Create or join leagues to compete against friends
- **Flexible League Formats**:
  - Season-long leagues for comprehensive competition
  - Week-to-week leagues for quick, focused matchups
- **Scoring System**: Track accuracy and earn points based on prediction correctness
- **Friend Challenges**: Invite friends and see how your predictions stack up against theirs
- **Real-Time Updates**: Live updates on game results and league standings
- **User Profiles**: Track your prediction history and statistics

## How It Works

1. **Sign Up**: Create an account and set up your profile
2. **Create or Join a League**: Start a new league or join an existing one with friends
3. **Make Predictions**: Forecast the outcomes of upcoming sporting events
4. **Track Results**: Watch games unfold and see your predictions validated
5. **Compete**: Rise through league rankings based on prediction accuracy
6. **Claim Victory**: Win your league and brag rights over your friends

## League Formats

### Season-Long Leagues
Compete throughout an entire sports season. Accumulate points across all games and events, with standings updated weekly.

### Week-to-Week Leagues
Quick competition format lasting a single week. Perfect for casual players or those who want shorter, focused competitions.
