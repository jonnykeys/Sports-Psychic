🏈 Correct Winner +10 PTS

Earn 10 base points for predicting the winning NFL team of each matchup correctly.

🎯 Closest Score Bonus +10 PTS

Contenders who pick the winning team compete for the Closest Score Bonus. The winning picker whose predicted score has the lowest absolute difference to the final score receives an additional +10 bonus points (or triples to +30 PTS if a 3X multiplier is used).

💡 If contenders tie for the closest score, the bonus is split evenly (e.g. +5 PTS each).
🔮 Exact Score Prediction +50 PTS

Correctly predict both the exact home and away final scores of the game! Hits on exact score award a massive 50-point jackpot (or 25 PTS each if tied; triples to 150 PTS on 3X multiplier).

⭐ Weekly Multiplier (3x) 3X MULTIPLIER

Each player designates one game per week as their confidence multiplier. If your pick wins, your winner points and closest/exact bonuses for that game are tripled!

💡 Max Payout: (10 base + 10 closest) × 3 = 60 PTS, or (10 base + 50 exact) × 3 = 180 PTS!
