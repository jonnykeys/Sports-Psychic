      Rules & Scoring System

      Detailed guide to how predictions, tie-breakers, multipliers, and bonuses are calculated.

      🏈 Regular Season Points

      • 10 Points: Awarded for every correct straight-up winning team picked across all 272 regular season games.
      • Score Proximity Tie-Breaker (+10 Bonus): Scores act as tie-breakers. Among all players who picked the winner, the player whose score prediction is closest to the actual final score wins a +10 bonus point (split evenly if tied).
      • 50 Points for Exact Score: Predicting the exact score of a game awards 50 points.
      • Weekly 3x Multiplier: You may select strictly ONE game per week for 3x points. This triples your win to 30 points, or a massive 150 points if you predict the exact score!

      🏁 End of Regular Season Points

      • 10 Points: For every NFL team whose final regular season win-loss record you projected correctly.
      • 10 Points: For every divisional record (e.g., 4-2) you projected correctly.
      • 50 Points: For correctly predicting the Regular Season NFL MVP.

      🏆 Playoff Points

      • 10 Points: For each playoff team picked correctly.
      • 30 Points: For every team that lands in the exact playoff seed you projected (Seeds 1–7).
      • 30 Points: For predicting a Super Bowl appearing team (60 points if both are correct).
      • 50 Points: For predicting the Super Bowl winner.
      • 250 Points: For predicting both Super Bowl teams AND the exact final score!
      • 50 Points: For predicting the Super Bowl MVP.
